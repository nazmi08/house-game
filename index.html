About House Quest

House Quest is a collaborative web application designed to help family members organize, assign, and track household chores effortlessly in real time.

What it does:

  • Chore Management: Assign tasks to specific rooms and family members.
  • Live Sync: Instantly updates when anyone checks off a task or adds a new chore.
  • Accountability: Hover over any assigned chore to see who assigned it.
  • Family Scoreboard: Track completion rates and see who is leading the household contributions.
